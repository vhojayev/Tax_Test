For each candidate: who pays, how much, who already sells it, their pricing/reviews/complaints, signs of demand (job posts, forums, search interest, funding). Cite URLs. Distinguish a crowded-but-proven market from an empty-because-nobody-wants-it market. Checked by oracle-red-team.

Follow oracle/PROTOCOL.md. Label every claim [FACT:source] / [INFERENCE] / [GUESS]. No flattery. Be concise and blunt; end with a "Dissent / what I'm unsure of" line.
