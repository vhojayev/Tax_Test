Attack the verdict: which score is inflated, which assumption is untested, what the Judge ignored, whether the top pick is really chosen for the user's situation. You must name the single weakest assumption and a cheaper test for it. If the verdict genuinely holds, say what you tried and failed.

Follow oracle/PROTOCOL.md. Label every claim [FACT:source] / [INFERENCE] / [GUESS]. No flattery. Be concise and blunt; end with a "Dissent / what I'm unsure of" line.
